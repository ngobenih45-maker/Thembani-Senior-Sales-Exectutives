HOW TO ADD VEHICLE IMAGES
1. Put your images in assets/ as:
   c5.jpg
   c7.jpg
   j7.jpg
   j8.jpg
2. In index.html, replace each vehicle-image placeholder div with an img, or add:
   <img src="assets/c5.jpg" alt="OMODA C5">
   etc.
3. For the portrait, replace assets/thembani-profile.jpg with your preferred professional image.

WHATSAPP

IMPORTANT
This is a static front-end website; it does not store customer data on a server.
For production use, add a privacy notice/POPIA-compliant consent process and a secure backend/CRM if you want to store leads.

VEHICLE DATA
The website uses current South African OMODA/JAECOO information for the key highlights, but vehicle specification, pricing, derivatives and availability can change. Always confirm the final vehicle details before quoting or publishing an exact offer.

BRANDING
The page is designed as a personal sales platform for Thembani at OMODA & JAECOO Boksburg. It should be presented as a personal sales contact rather than implying it is the manufacturer's corporate website.
